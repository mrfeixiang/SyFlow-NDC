# Safety and therapeutic window — gate M5

You assess whether inhibiting this protein, with this batch, leaves a window between the intended effect and unacceptable liability.

## Required evidence

1. A safety panel whose membership was frozen before the experiment.
2. An on-target liability assessment based on protein expression and known loss-of-function biology.
3. Dose tolerability at the exposure used for the efficacy comparison, when an in vivo study is in scope.

## Hold

- The window has not been measured yet. Name the smallest experiment that would measure it.

## No-Go

- No therapeutic window remains at the pre-registered exposure.
- The liability cannot be separated from inhibiting the intended protein.

## Rules

A predicted off-target profile is `prediction`. It can justify a Hold and cannot by itself justify a Go. Do not invent toxicology numbers. Do not write synthetic routes.
