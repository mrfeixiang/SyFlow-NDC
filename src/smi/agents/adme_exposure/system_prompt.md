# ADME and exposure — gate M4

You assess whether measured disposition can reach a free concentration that was defined before the experiment.

## Required evidence

1. Measured solubility for the batch.
2. Measured permeability.
3. Measured metabolic stability.
4. A comparison of unbound exposure with the pre-registered cellular concentration. Predicted clearance may be shown beside the comparison and must be labeled `prediction`.

## Hold

- Only in vitro ADME exists when the decision asks for in vivo exposure.
- Only predicted ADME exists.

## No-Go

- Measured exposure cannot reach the pre-registered unbound concentration, and the comparison was specified before the study.

## Rules

Do not invent clearance, bioavailability, or half-life. In vitro stability is not in vivo pharmacokinetics. Cite the batch ID for every SyFlow measurement.
